# ThreadSanitizer suppressions
#
# Intentionally empty. Dependencies built from source (oneTBB and mimalloc)
# are instrumented on TSan legs, and first-party cross-thread delivery avoids
# transient Qt functor objects. Do not add module-wide patterns such as
# race:*Qt* or race:*tbb*: they also hide application races whose stacks pass
# through those frameworks. Any unavoidable platform false positive must be
# captured with its full stack and suppressed by an exact top-level symbol.
